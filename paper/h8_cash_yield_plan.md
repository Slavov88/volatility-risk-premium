Treasury scenario accrual is a **modeled liquid cash account** at the prior-open-known rate, simple ACT/365F: cash_income=actual post-trade prior-opening cash × r × actual calendar days/365. Quote units converted once from percent. Prior cash remains unchanged until next open, then income is credited before dividend credit and the original rebalance. Rate is fixed for that open-to-open interval, including weekends/holidays; no intragap daily rate updates or compounding. Interest compounds through subsequent credited balances. ACT/365F is an explicitly chosen cash-proxy convention, not a claim about coupon accrual/actual Treasury total returns. Daily cash day count uses calendar dates (including leap day), not DST clock hours. First row starts funded at the first original open, earns no pre-inception interest; final open receives the last interval interest and old-share dividend, no final rebalance/liquidation. Interest may change shares/traded notional/fees at identical target exposures; keeping dollar trades fixed would violate self-financing. Taxes, sweep fees, Treasury execution spreads/mark-to-market, payment-date financing and guaranteed broker remuneration remain unmodeled.
