# Editorial review

## Release-blocking or material corrections

1. Appendix C says “six unresolved price discrepancies” but the interim amendment registers **seven** discrepancy dates. The later source check appears to resolve one of the seven: clarify explicitly that all seven were registered and checked, and identify which six remain unresolved and why; otherwise the two counts appear contradictory. Do not change six to seven without checking resolution status.
2. H3 calls values inside its confidence interval “economically meaningful” without defining or defending an economic materiality threshold. Replace with “The interval spans both an advantage and a disadvantage on the squared-loss scale.” Statistical uncertainty alone cannot establish economic magnitude.
3. Portfolio paragraph compares buy-and-hold CAGR with risk-targeted returns without the like-risk qualification. Add “Buy-and-hold has different realized risk and is not a like-risk comparator.” The cash figures are sensitivity scenarios, not realizable promised cash returns.
4. The numerical ledger only covers core A–I. Add exact keys/table selectors and hashes for return diagnostics, option validation and additional quadrature/strip checks, alternate-close GK, replay and all extension claims. The accompanying claim audit identifies the two still-unpinned computations; do not imply complete numerical traceability until they are pinned.
5. Final H1/H2/H5 analysis is explicitly provisional despite independent arithmetic checks. Keep that caveat in abstract and conclusion; distinguish author-reported consent and later operational execution authorization from independent acceptance. Missing original acquisition manifest, complete FRED response and historical fit database prevent claiming a complete raw-to-final historical replay.

## Wording and coverage

- Title says “equity index markets” while confirmatory evidence is S&P 500/VIX only. Recommend “The Volatility Risk Premium: Evidence from S&P 500 Implied and Realized Variance” (with theoretical appendix as appropriate).
- Abstract accurately reports H1, corrected H3 and conditional H4, with confidence interval and review status. “The NBER recession shift is also imprecise” is appropriate; no equality inference. “Later samples and alternative realized-variance estimators materially qualify” is defensible, but recent H1 non-rejection should not be called disappearance or a break.
- Explicitly name the H1 inference two-sided, and identify the 95% H3 interval as HAC in abstract if space allows. H1 p and H2 Wald are already in the body.
- “These estimates reject a zero VIX contribution” should be “reject the null of a zero VIX coefficient conditional on GARCH”; a coefficient is predictive association, not causation. Avoid “useful option-market information” implying tradable profits.
- At H1, 86% positive supports the descriptive frequency but does not quantify the contribution of tails to the mean. Replace “rather than being attributable solely to a small count of unusually large positive values” with “The positive fraction describes frequency, while the mean also depends on magnitudes.”
- Return diagnostics report only some protocol-required quantities in prose/figure. Supply an appendix table for Jarque–Bera, Ljung–Box of returns, quantiles, and ACF or explicitly point to a preserved supplement; pre-GARCH plots should include return time series and histogram/density. Report standardized GARCH residual and remaining ARCH diagnostics or explain unavailability. State whether “all fits converged” refers only to numerical convergence.
- The robustness paragraph notes the fixed 21-day forecast invariance, but should give accessible complete H3/H4 results by estimator, window, distribution, period and lag in supplement. H5 42/63 lag sensitivities and H2 estimator/horizon comparisons also need an accessible complete table. The manuscript cannot substitute broad assertions for the protocol's mandatory hierarchy.
- Discussion rightly separates risk-neutral price and physical forecast, measurement basis, and compensation. Specify that inference is for the unconditional historical sample mean, not proof of every-period positive expected premium. H4 rolling p=.04846 is a sensitivity near a conventional threshold, not a stable reversal.
- The literature and theory provide a sound coherent mechanism and explicit jump/monitoring limitations. Verify every external factual statement against primary source and pin the exact report for independent numerical checks. Use “synthetic checks” rather than “market validation” for the six numerical diffusion examples.
- Extension correctly labels its work exploratory, specifies baseline/forecast comparisons and retains the missing cross-market multiplicity/bootstrap limitation. Explain 1.5 threshold was selected after the core and distinguish ex post overlapping event origins from distinct events. The registered full cash proxy is an assumption and the comparison cannot establish variance-selling compensation.
- Appendix C's 72 analyses and 1,134 decisions are arithmetic checks, not independent original fit reproductions. Preserve this distinction and supply exact receipts; avoid presenting the public package as complete while its upstream chain remains open.
