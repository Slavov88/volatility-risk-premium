## Findings

1. **Scientific acceptance remains open.** The strict Cboe acquisition gate failed, the interim CLOSE pathway is author-reported, and the operational authorization to execute H1–H5 before review does not constitute independent acceptance. Original SPX acquisition manifest and original FRED SP500 validation response are missing; fresh daily and target file hashes differ from the originals. The manuscript discloses these limitations. Its results must retain provisional status; passing tests, numerical reconstructions and this review cannot supply coauthor acceptance.
2. **Required reporting is incomplete in the manuscript as inspected.** Protocol §§9 and 11 require, among other outputs, Jarque–Bera and return Ljung–Box, return quantiles and time series/histogram, VRP quantiles and ACF, and GARCH parameters, likelihood, standardized-residual diagnostics and remaining ARCH effects. The present manuscript includes only some diagnostics and convergence/persistence. Supply a traceable supplement covering these items and point to it, or clearly designate them outstanding. A test-suite pass does not provide the required scientific reporting.
3. **Replay grades are different.** Recovery of the three frozen panel CSV hashes and two matching fresh builds is supported by the provenance receipt; derived daily/targets are not historical byte matches, and the original full manifest chain cannot be verified. Fresh GARCH refits used the producer fitting implementation, not independent GARCH code; matching masks and unchanged threshold classifications are a numerical aggregate comparison, not historical cache replay. Independent NumPy H3/H4 primary inference is a check of the new fits, not independent model fitting. The manuscript should preserve each qualification whenever describing replication.
4. **Cross-market inference incomplete.** The previous audit identified omitted registered paired block bootstrap and H1–H5 Holm family, alongside hardcoded paired HAC21. The manuscript appropriately calls this exploratory and does not claim a cross-market difference or equivalence. A HAC interval cannot substitute for the missing registered analysis.
5. **Source discrepancies:** seven alternate-close scenarios plus joint scenario were examined; original FRED response is unavailable. The language “six unresolved price discrepancies” in Appendix C should be reconciled explicitly with the seven tested dates, ideally identifying which discrepancy is resolved and evidence, rather than letting the numbers appear contradictory.

## Checks and limits

- Previous numeric audit checked arithmetic on 72 H1, 486 H3 and 486 H4 table rows; it did not validate the underlying observation-level inference.
- Fresh comparison receipt states 72 cells, all common mask hashes/counts match, and zero 5% classification changes; it is explicitly labeled fresh numerical comparison, not cache replay. Fifteen leakage anchors do not prove a full-origin independent replay.
- The integration test log reports 1,839 passed; the corrected-branch provenance test log reports 1,816 passed. These are historical logs, not reruns by this reviewer; their scopes must not be conflated with external data acceptance or exact replay.
- The core narrative generally distinguishes ex-post proxy from conditional premium, calibration from forecast ranking, and sample ranking from inference, and flags the required rolling-GARCH sensitivity. Those statements remain conditional on the provisional data and checks above.
