## Recommendations for final integration

1. **Qualify the horizon in the abstract and introduction.** Replace “realized variance over the exact following 30 calendar days” with “a realized-variance proxy formed from returns ending within the following 30 calendar days.” The window and annualization follow the protocol exactly, but a non-trading endpoint leaves the final partial interval unobserved; appendix A already explains this. Likewise “exact calendar target respects” can become “the calendar-window target is aligned to.” No data or specification change is proposed.
2. **Make assumptions explicit at the first SDF identity.** Define \(\mathcal V\) as the same integrable, annualized payoff in both expectations, \(M\) as the positive terminal pricing kernel, and \(Q\) as the measure induced by the normalized kernel. Deterministic discounting makes \(K_{var}=E_t^Q\mathcal V\). Without it, use the maturity-forward measure, or discounted-expectation ratio already stated in appendix A. The current covariance sign and the long buyer's expected payoff are correct under these assumptions; avoid interpreting a positive covariance as an empirically identified mechanism.
3. **Bibliography deduplication.** In the inspected snapshot the 2014 circular, 2026 versioned methodology and historical data page are three distinct sources, not duplicates. Retain all three; remove any extra generic entry referring to the same methodology PDF if another integration branch introduces one. Cite the current methodology as Cboe Global Indices (2026), the historical page as Cboe (n.d.), and the circular as Cboe (2014). The separately prepared `bibliography.md` is an alphabetized copy of this snapshot's reference list; check it again against any subsequent manuscript edits.
4. **Preserve provisional status.** The deviation log records author-reported authorization of the interim pathway and later permission for provisional execution, while independent freeze and coauthor scientific acceptance remain pending. Neither this review nor numerical validation constitutes that acceptance. Keep the stated historical conflict and full-replay limitations.
5. **Use “variance-proxy forecast” where practical.** VIX-versus-GARCH loss assesses the stipulated daily-return target, not necessarily true latent continuous variance. Patton (2011) establishes specific conditions under which a loss ranking is robust to proxy noise; it does not imply arbitrary biased proxies are interchangeable. The current wording generally respects this distinction.

## Mathematical checks

- \(I=(VIX/100)^2\) and \(Y=(365/30)\sum r_d^2\) are annualized decimal variance; the volatility gap is in annualized decimal volatility. The observation-level factorization and warning that average signs need not agree are correct. The \(Q-P\) premium gives long expected payoff \(-VRP\), and the manuscript correctly reverses Carr–Wu's realized-minus-swap-rate convention.
- The four-term decomposition telescopes exactly for a matched \(\mathcal V^*\). Physical forecast error has conditional mean zero under integrability and the actual conditional expectation; measurement and index-replication bases need not.
- The Black–Scholes–Merton PDE, prices, parity, vega, exercise threshold and Gaussian expectation are consistent with the stated continuous dividend-yield convention. Exact continuous-strip replication requires continuous paths, suitable integrability, true-martingale and trading conditions, and an ideal continuum of strikes. A cash index is not itself literally a tradable underlying; the replication is a frictionless benchmark for attainable exposure, not a claim that one can trade the published index.
- The log-contract strip, discounting factor, annualization and jump discrepancy sign are correct. Cboe's strike-grid formula and interpolation of total variance rather than volatilities are consistent with the stated methodology. The range formulas, HAC sandwich and negative squared-loss differential favoring VIX are algebraically consistent. This review does not independently reproduce the empirical numbers or establish HAC finite-sample coverage.
- `check_theory.py` correctly checks six European prices by Gaussian quadrature and six idealized diffusion strips, and checks six supplied quotes against spread and IV tolerance. Those are model implementation checks, not a historical VIX replication, an empirical jump test, or independent source validation. Attempting `PYTHONPATH=repo/src python theory-delivery/check_theory.py` in the default environment failed on missing `exchange_calendars` before executing checks; the previously recorded receipt was not independently regenerated here.
