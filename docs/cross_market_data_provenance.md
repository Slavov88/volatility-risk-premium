## Cash yield inputs used by H8

The cash-yield replay reuses the Task 3 initial-release snapshots and availability rules from [`h8_cash_rate_source_v1.json`](../data/manifests/h8_cash_rate_source_v1.json), SHA-256 `61ff6f555ea3d6c1106b4717ee83f215b17e379ad8cea78852637b7d216f91d7`. DGS1MO is a quoted one-month Treasury constant-maturity par yield; it is not an overnight cash return or a broker sweep rate. DTB4WK is a quoted four-week Treasury bill investment-rate series and is converted under the existing registered yield-convention routine. Each observation is usable only after its initial release and is locked for the next interval; cash accrues on the previous balance for actual elapsed calendar days under ACT/365. Missing rate history is not invented or silently filled. The base, half-rate, and DTB4WK alternatives preserve the same fixed QQQ signals. All scenarios are sensitivity assumptions rather than guaranteed investable cash compensation.

## Russell 2000 and EURO STOXX 50 gate

The official Cboe RVX index history is present and validated for 2009-09-16–2026-10-14; FRED RVXCLS begins 2004-01-02 and matches the Cboe series exactly across 4,290 shared observations. A full-history Russell 2000 underlying-index source that passed the independent source-authority gate was not present. The environment's ^RUT vendor snapshot is retained only as a candidate, not used for H1–H7; an IWM ETF cannot silently stand in for the index's realized-variance target.

The EURO STOXX 50/VSTOXX pathway is not executed. STOXX's technical information says full historical index-value files are available to license holders, with shorter public history; a locally entitled full-history file, its coverage, and exact venue calendar/time-zone alignment were not available for validation. An ETF proxy without matched index and implied-volatility history cannot identify the registered H1–H8 estimands. See [STOXX historical-file access details](https://www.stoxx.com/document/News/2026/April/Technical_Information_STOXX_Index_intraday_snapshot_final_20260410.pdf), [STOXX license conditions](https://stoxx.com/legal/stoxx-conditions-of-use/), and the [LSEG historical index-value portal](https://www.lseg.com/en/ftse-russell/index-resources/historic-index-values) for source-access context.

The data gate is not a finding about the markets' underlying economics. It is an execution blocker. No Russell or European null, effect size, ranking, or forecast result is asserted.
