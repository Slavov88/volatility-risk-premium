# Literature review evidence map

**EXPLORATORY — POST H1–H5 RESULT SELECTION — PENDING INDEPENDENT REVIEW**

Question: can a small as-of model family add useful risk information beyond implied and historical variance, and does that information survive realistic SPY execution? Focused design/contribution audit, not systematic review; English primary academic/provider sources accessed 2026-10-13. Search clusters: VRP/physical calibration; HAR/EWMA/GARCH; scoring/proxy noise; managed portfolios/implementation. Abstract-only access is explicitly limited below; no exhaustive novelty verdict.

Settled: implied/physical variance distinction, simple persistent-volatility models, proper scores, and volatility scaling all have substantial precedents. Uncertain in our setting: incremental warning quality at an explicit alert budget, mature-label combinations versus strong constituents, and whether forecast changes reduce capped SPY risk error net of costs. Forecast combination itself is established methodology; no invention claim is warranted. Our strongest possible contribution is a transparent boundary/replication study linking target-maturity discipline, probabilistic underestimation alerts, loss-function rankings, and a self-financing investable execution test on one frozen history.

Gap verdict: **partly holds as a narrow empirical extension; novelty not established**. Publication bias is addressed by retaining the Cederburg counterevidence and all model/cost/period failures. We do not infer absence of other work from this focused search. Implementation shortfall includes delay, spread, impact and opportunity cost; flat traded-notional costs below are sensitivity assumptions and cannot establish historical executable fills. Provider/contract data limitations are audited separately in H9.

Required narrative: explain noisy proxy and risk-neutral calibration first; then small benchmark families and mature-label fitting; then compare statistical gains with realized risk control and costs. Claims that must be earned: stronger calibrated warnings, constituent-beating forecasts, net implementable risk control. Neither method novelty nor generalizable alpha is assumed.
